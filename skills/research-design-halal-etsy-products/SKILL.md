# Halal Etsy Product Research & Design

## PART 1 — CORE SKILL

### Objective

Find halal digital products with strong evidence of current Etsy buying demand, distinguish viable entry opportunities, and create original improvements supported by customer needs.

Include suitable general-purpose products; do not limit discovery to Islamic-themed downloads.

Preserve this objective when choosing tools, agents and outputs.

### Route the request

**Research.** Follow the research protocol in Part 3. Deliver evidence-backed rankings; do not manufacture products merely to fill a report.

**Design.** Follow the design protocol in Part 4. Use the supplied idea or brief; identify unvalidated market assumptions without forcing a new market study.

**Complete.** Research, select a supported opportunity, produce its brief, build and verify the product, then prepare its listing package. Proceed through reversible work when authorised. Ask only when a material unresolved choice prevents useful progress.

**Improve or continue.** Read available project records and the relevant artifacts. Preserve completed work. Never claim access to an unavailable record.

Always apply the business profile and halal/originality protocols. Use the records protocol to structure research, design and continuation records.

### Apply the business profile

Use explicit user instructions and approved available records first.

Ask only for missing settings that materially affect the task. Continue independent work while awaiting answers where possible.

Prefer free research and production tools.

Do not infer the target market from timezone.

Without a specified country, make an explicitly provisional English-language discovery pass without claiming local demand, tax compliance or a universal market.

Choose print sizes and software requirements explicitly before final production.

### Evidence and tool discipline

- Before collecting evidence or stating any Etsy rule, test access to Etsy search results, listing pages, listing reviews and Etsy Help, and record which were reachable. Treat Etsy rules seen only in search snippets or third-party summaries as "unverified at source" until read on Etsy's own page or confirmed by the user from the live seller form.
- Browse for current Etsy facts, listings, policies, tool capabilities and seller success claims.
- Cite material claims; record evidence periods, retrieval dates and limitations.
- Prefer accessible Etsy first-party demand data.
- Discover service-specific tools before browser fallback and follow access rules.
- Use public search for public information research.
- Never imply access to Shop Manager, Marketplace Insights, private orders or paid databases that was not actually available.
- Ask for a relevant export when necessary; meanwhile label public-only findings provisional and continue accessible work.
- Verify free-plan limits before quoting them.
- Use Etsy search/reviews, Marketplace Insights when available, eRank Free, Google Trends and optional Pinterest Trends according to the question.
- Keep evidence in a simple table or spreadsheet.
- Label third-party figures as estimates unless verified.
- Never infer precise units sold from reviews, shop sales or favourites.
- Keep keyword demand, product purchase evidence and entry opportunity distinct.
- Do not promise exact bestseller rankings, certain profitability or halal certification.
- Match conclusion strength to evidence.

### Verify Etsy rules in the user's browser

When a browser tool that acts in the user's own browser is available (for example Claude in Chrome), use it for the access check and to read Etsy's own pages; follow that tool's own guidance first. Pages read this way count as read at source: record the URL, retrieval date and the exact rule.

Before production or listing work, check on Etsy's own pages:

- Digital-listing files: file count, per-file size and accepted file types (including XLSX and ZIP).
- Creativity Standards and production attributes for the planned production method.
- AI-disclosure requirements and any restrictions on AI-generated digital items.
- Listing-image requirements for digital items.
- Title and tag limits.
- Current seller fees for the user's country.

Read public help, policy and listing pages only. Do not sign in, change shop or account settings, create or edit listings, buy, message anyone, or open Shop Manager unless the user asks for that specific action. If the user asks you to read their Shop Manager or Marketplace Insights data, read only what they asked for and do not record customer personal data.

When no such browser tool is available, keep the snippet-only rules marked "unverified at source" and ask the user to confirm them on the live listing form.

### Delegate substantial research

Use specialist agents when available and authorised; keep simple work in one thread.

Assign non-overlapping tasks and a common market, date range and product definition:

1. Demand researcher: keyword clusters, seasonality and accessible first-party data.
2. Purchase-evidence researcher: product-specific activity across independent sellers.
3. Competition/customer researcher: entrants, prices, unmet needs and documented seller methods.
4. Evidence/halal reviewer: contradictions, source independence, suitability and originality concerns.

Use production specialists after defining the brief.

Require sources, observations, uncertainty and deliverables.

Retain responsibility for synthesis; agent agreement is not independent evidence.

Honour tool-specific citation requirements for agent sources.

Do not contact sellers or customers without explicit authorisation.

### Apply separate gates

Do not use an arbitrary weighted total to hide a failed requirement:

1. **Demand:** relevant Etsy searches and/or recent purchase signals, with gaps labelled.
2. **Entry:** plausible buyer-specific advantage and competition assessment.
3. **Suitability:** halal screen, asset provenance and Etsy eligibility. Before shortlisting, record the intended production method (original, licensed, AI-assisted, AI-generated) and check it against Etsy's current Creativity Standards, AI rules and listing-image requirements for that product type. If those cannot be read at source, record Etsy eligibility as "unverified" separately from the halal status, and do not assign either Suitable status overall until eligibility is verified.
4. **Feasibility:** ability to produce and test the actual promised formats in the available environments, within current Etsy file-count, file-size and file-type limits.
5. **Economics:** realistic prices, applicable fees, support effort and assumptions.

For complete requests, select the strongest supported candidate and explain the trade-off.

If evidence cannot support selection, deliver the provisional shortlist and smallest useful validation step.

Build an explicitly unvalidated prototype only when authorised; never call it a validated winner.

### Complete and preserve the work

**Research.** Deliver separate demand and entry rankings with dates, sources, contradictions and confidence. State accessible data and actual observation windows.

**Design.** Deliver usable files and editable sources where feasible, visually and functionally checked, with instructions, provenance and honest compatibility statements. Do not stop at a mockup when a working product was requested.

**Complete.** Include research summary, brief, verified files and listing package.

Save user-facing artifacts through the available approved storage workflow.

Save reusable skill instructions through the available skill-management process.

Never overwrite unrelated files.

Record profile assumptions, evidence, decisions, artifact locations, checks and next step.

Do not store customer personal data unnecessarily or write project-specific evidence back into reusable skill instructions.

Do not publish, buy assets, run paid ads or schedule later research merely because this skill was invoked. Obtain appropriate authorisation.

Do not imply later observations happen automatically.

Report outcomes, files, meaningful checks and limitations concisely. Separate facts, hypotheses and recommendations.

## PART 2 — BUSINESS PROFILE

### Established preferences

- Focus on Etsy digital products with strong current buying demand and realistic entry opportunities.
- Require halal suitability in content, intended use and marketing.
- Include suitable general-purpose products.
- Improve customer outcomes through original work, not copied designs or superficial variations.
- Prefer free tools.
- Do not spend money without authorisation.
- Use evidence-based decisions, explicit uncertainty and reusable research/design workflows.

### Configurable settings

**Customer country, currency and language.** Until specified, conduct provisional English-language discovery and record the country as unspecified.

**Product formats.** Investigate PDFs/printables, spreadsheets and editable templates; select on evidence and feasibility.

**Halal interpretation.** Screen clearly prohibited intended uses. Mark interpretation-sensitive features unresolved. Use suitable neutral alternatives for reversible drafts. Seek relevant user requirements or qualified guidance before final inclusion.

**Scholarly guidance followed.** Unspecified. Do not attribute a school or ruling to the user.

**Design identity.** Use readable, accessible layouts. Do not invent a logo or business claims.

**Software.** Prefer free tools and portable files. Disclose buyer software/account requirements.

**Budget, time and income goal.** Unknown. Use transparent scenarios instead of invented targets.

**Publishing.** Prepare deliverables; publish only when explicitly authorised.

Ask only for settings needed for current work.

Do not hold discovery for brand colours.

Resolve relevant requirements before finalising Arabic religious text, disputed imagery, regional financial calculations or an incompatible format.

Keep profile changes in the approved project record.

Update reusable skill instructions only when asked to change the skill.

## PART 3 — ETSY PRODUCT RESEARCH PROTOCOL

### Define the comparison

Record:

- Research date.
- Market.
- Language.
- Currency.
- Timeframe.
- Access limits, including the access-check result: which of Etsy search, listing pages, reviews, Etsy Help and user-supplied first-party data were available.
- Halal requirements.

If listing pages or reviews cannot be retrieved, do not rank purchase evidence from snippets or third-party summaries. Request user-supplied screenshots, saved pages or exports for named listings, deliver keyword discovery labelled "purchase evidence not collected", and continue other accessible work.

Before broad discovery, compare the planned queries per candidate against the verified free quotas of each tool. Allocate the same query budget to every candidate being ranked. If quotas prevent equal coverage, reduce candidates or split work across user-run sessions and record the split. Do not rank candidates measured with different tool budgets.

Define each candidate as: **Buyer + task + format + software/print requirements.**

Separate printable planners, tablet planners and spreadsheets.

Group colour variants as one offer family unless evidence supports a distinct use.

For broad discovery, scan roughly 10–15 product families, then deepen a small shortlist.

For a specified niche or candidate comparison, investigate the requested scope directly.

Treat ranges as planning aids, not statistical guarantees.

Avoid limiting discovery to previously suggested categories.

Screen unsuitable and Etsy-ineligible products early.

### Collect demand evidence

Build buyer-intent keyword clusters from suggestions, listings and accessible tools.

Prefer Etsy first-party search data when available.

Treat Etsy Marketplace Insights, run by the user and shared, as first-party search data. Before use, verify its current geography, window and quota on Etsy Help. Record the geography as reported and the window. Do not treat it as country-specific, or as evidence of seasonality or persistence, without dated repeat pulls or a longer-period source.

Record:

- Geography.
- Period.
- Source.
- Retrieval date.
- Observed or estimated status.

Rank demand only on keywords that match the candidate definition. When only broader parent keywords are available, report them as context, mark candidate-specific demand unknown, and do not place the candidate in the ordinal demand ranking on that basis.

Do not sum related keywords as unique buyers or extrapolate monthly searches into annual sales.

Treat autocomplete as keyword discovery, not proof of search volume, exact buyer phrasing or purchases.

Corroborate important terms with accessible Etsy demand data and product evidence.

Verify dates and scope on the underlying source where possible. Search snippets can be stale or incomplete. Label snippet-only evidence and do not use it alone for a high-confidence conclusion.

Use Google Trends for long-term interest and seasonality.

Use Pinterest Trends for visual hypotheses.

Neither establishes Etsy unit sales.

Label missing or low-volume data unknown rather than zero demand.

Keep searches, purchases and entry opportunity separate.

A searches/listings ratio is a discovery clue, not conversion probability.

Inspect actual listing relevance rather than relying on headline counts.

### Sample listings consistently

Begin with about 20 relevant listings across 10 independent shops per promising niche when accessible.

Include leaders, smaller/newer shops and weaker comparable offers.

Expand or narrow based on evidence and constraints.

Disclose actual coverage and selection method.

Use a consistent retrieval and selection method across candidates and disclose departures.

This deliberately varied sample supports qualitative analysis, not estimates of market share.

Do not aggregate sampled reviews or count active sellers as estimates of niche-wide demand.

Label separately discovered examples.

Record:

- Product-specific review dates and counts.
- Actual price and currency.
- Contents.
- Requirements.
- Shop age.
- Known product history.
- Differentiation.
- Customer feedback.

Label shop-wide lifetime figures separately.

Do not treat refreshed listing dates as launches or purchases.

Exclude physical goods, unrelated reviews and different formats from digital-product comparisons.

Prioritise purchase signals from the latest 30–90 days where available, with relevant annual seasons.

Label older evidence historical.

Reviews support buying activity but do not reveal total transactions or profit.

Absence of reviews does not prove no sales.

Search positions are personalised and may include ads; first results are not necessarily bestsellers.

Use a common observation window and comparable evidence scope for candidates being ranked.

Record whether recent-review coverage is complete or partial.

Do not compare raw counts across unequal windows, samples or access coverage. Narrow the comparison, use qualified qualitative findings, or report a tie/insufficient evidence rather than force an ordinal ranking.

Account for review publication lag; review dates do not establish exact transaction dates.

### Audit search-result competition

For important buyer queries, inspect a consistently defined set of relevant visible results when available.

Record:

- Query.
- Date.
- Market/location settings if known.
- Result coverage.
- Visible ad labels.
- Shop/listing identifiers.
- Relevant smaller-seller examples.

Treat this as a visibility snapshot, not a representative market census.

Separate labelled ads from organic results.

Ad visibility does not establish advertising spend or organic dominance.

Do not automatically exclude a niche using arbitrary shop-review thresholds, the absence of a recent visible review, or an unsupported monopoly percentage.

Qualify concentration as within the observed sample.

Missing recent evidence triggers an uncertainty/seasonality check.

Retain, deprioritise or reject with reasons from combined evidence, not a single cutoff.

### Repeat observations honestly

Capture a baseline.

When later runs actually occur, revisit the same listing IDs around two and four weeks later.

Record timestamps, dated review evidence, price changes and contents changes.

Do not assume review display order is chronological.

Deliver the current snapshot now. State that longitudinal evidence is pending.

Do not:

- Wait weeks to provide the current result.
- Fabricate observations.
- Schedule tasks without authorisation.
- Promise monitoring without an actual authorised mechanism.

Distinguish sustained, seasonal, emerging and historical demand.

### Extract customer and success evidence

Analyse positive and critical reviews for outcomes, purchase reasons, compatibility issues, instructions, accessibility and unmet needs.

Seek repetition across buyers and shops.

Do not generalise from one complaint.

Obtain authorisation before contacting people.

Study seller accounts for practices and timing.

Distinguish revenue from profit, self-reported from verified, and historical from current results.

Note known existing audience, advertising, labour, product mix and promotional interests.

Do not infer a tactic caused growth or that course-seller results are typical.

Compare weaker offers without inventing why they failed.

### Challenge and screen

Check for:

- Duplicate underlying data.
- Keyword overlap.
- Market mismatch.
- Stale reviews.
- Shop/product confusion.
- Ads/organic ambiguity.
- Discount dependence.

Investigate searches without buying evidence and activity concentrated in one seller.

Apply halal/originality checks.

Calculate economics using applicable verified fees, realistic prices, support/customisation effort and disclosed creation-cost assumptions.

Do not invent forecasts.

Missing entrant evidence is uncertainty, not proof entry is impossible.

Require entrant evidence to concern the relevant product and observed purchase activity.

Shop age alone establishes neither product launch date, acquisition difficulty nor attainable success.

Mark unknown product history unknown.

### Deliver two rankings

**1. Strongest observed demand.** Consider relevant searches, recent purchase signals, distribution across sellers, persistence and seasonality.

**2. Best entry opportunities.** Consider demand confidence, meaningful differentiation, entrant evidence, economics and feasibility.

Include:

- Specific product and buyer.
- Evidence period.
- Price and currency.
- Sample.
- Confidence.
- Contradictions.
- Sources.

Separate observations, estimates and recommendations.

Use reasoned ordinal judgement, not an invented predictive score.

Assign confidence separately to demand and entry:

- **Higher:** independent recent signals agree, coverage is adequate, and repeated observations exist where needed.
- **Moderate:** signals are corroborated but access, coverage or observation time is incomplete.
- **Low:** evidence depends on one shop, old reviews, mostly estimates or material contradictions.

Never claim a comprehensive exact bestseller leaderboard from public data.

Finish with:

- Recommendation.
- Strongest reason it might fail.
- Smallest useful check.

Stop deepening weak candidates when more evidence is unlikely to change selection. Investigate unresolved contenders when it could.

## PART 4 — ORIGINAL PRODUCT DESIGN AND DELIVERY

### Define the brief

Use the record structure in Part 6.

State:

- Buyer.
- Task.
- Outcome.
- Supporting evidence.
- Original customer-backed improvement.
- Contents.
- Exclusions.
- Source/export formats.
- Dimensions.
- Requirements.
- Customer file map and delivery route within current Etsy limits.
- Verification environments.
- Acceptance checks.

Label unvalidated assumptions.

Improve concrete results:

- Setup speed.
- Calculation reliability.
- Teaching sequence.
- Accessibility.
- Localisation.
- Editing.
- Instructions.

Do not copy competitors' content, layouts or assets.

Do not use paid competitor files as starting templates without authorisation and rights.

### Select tools and formats

Read applicable authoring guidance before creating spreadsheets, PDFs, documents or presentations.

Use image generation for requested raster visuals, with provenance/content checks.

Use precise tools for diagrams, functional layouts, spelling, Arabic and calculations.

Do not rely on generated-image text for accuracy.

Prefer free tools and portable files.

Provide editable source and customer exports where feasible.

Do not promise Canva, Adobe or Goodnotes native links without actual authorised access and working deliverables.

Offer a clearly identified supported alternative when needed.

Disclose premium elements and buyer account/software requirements.

Obtain authorisation before spending.

Before production, record the verification environment for each promised format (for example, spreadsheet engine, PDF renderer/reader, installed fonts).

Prefer spreadsheet functions that exist in every application the listing will name, or list the unsupported applications.

Before Arabic production, confirm an Arabic-capable font is installed and its licence permits embedding or redistribution. If none is available, stop the Arabic portion and record it as blocked.

### Verify actual deliverables

**Spreadsheet**

- Exercise normal, blank, zero, invalid and boundary inputs.
- Independently calculate expected results.
- Verify totals, dates, currency, protection and editable cells.
- Remove private/sample data or separate labelled examples.
- Test claimed formats/platforms where feasible.

**Printable/PDF**

- Render and inspect every page.
- Check clipping, margins, fonts, contrast, writing space, dimensions and counts.
- Inspect each promised size.
- Test low-ink versions if promised.
- Do not claim physical printing unless performed.

**Fillable PDF**

- Check fields, tab order, saved values, labels and reader compatibility.
- Distinguish from a static PDF.

**Editable template**

- Check clean-copy editing.
- Check permissions.
- Check fonts/assets.
- Check links and every promised export.

**Educational material**

- Verify facts, level, instructions, answer keys and progression.
- Proofread Arabic direction, shaping, diacritics and letters.
- Obtain qualified review for religious instruction and unresolved claims.

**All formats**

- Check spelling.
- Open files.
- Check filenames, links and contents.
- Check instructions.
- Check accessibility.
- Check asset rights.
- Record the engine, reader or environment that produced each result. Do not claim Excel, Google Sheets, Adobe Reader, Goodnotes, Canva or mobile compatibility from a different application's result. Mark a check that could not run as blocked, not passed.

Record actual checks and untested compatibility.

Fix material defects.

Do not call mockups functioning spreadsheets or editable templates.

Check spreadsheet error states and circular references without masking invalid calculations behind blank output or indiscriminate error suppression.

Remove unintended private metadata while preserving necessary attribution, provenance and licence notices.

Describe products as tested against documented criteria, not technically bulletproof or error-free under every possible use.

Using the final delivery package and start guide, complete one representative buyer task from opening/setup through saving, exporting or printing. Use a clean copy where relevant. Record the outcome, fix material friction and disclose unavailable platform checks.

For printables, state intended paper size, orientation and scaling. Include trim/bleed instructions only when needed.

### Package and prepare the listing

Include:

- Product files.
- Editable sources where appropriate.
- Start guide.
- Contents.
- Requirements.
- Permitted-use terms aligned with rights and provenance.

Keep internal competitor evidence outside customer downloads.

Before production, and again before packaging, verify Etsy's current digital-listing file count, per-file size and supported file types on Etsy's official help or the live listing form. Do not assume source formats such as XLSX upload directly. If a needed type is not supported, deliver it inside a ZIP or through a verified link in a delivered PDF, and record the route used.

Use authorised, verified delivery links only as needed.

Check the assembled customer download against the brief and listing:

- Include every promised file, size and version.
- Use clear filenames.
- Verify archives extract when used.
- Exclude internal working materials.

Distinguish production sources delivered to the business owner from editable formats offered to customers.

Redistribute only what the offer promises and the asset licences permit.

Inspect archive contents for unintended system artifacts such as "__MACOSX" or ".DS_Store" and remove them from customer packages.

Test extraction in available environments. Claim Windows, macOS or mobile checks only when actually performed.

Check download/setup instructions against Etsy's current buyer guidance.

For complete/listing requests prepare:

- Accurate title.
- Description.
- Relevant tags/categories.
- Original previews of actual contents/results.
- The Etsy production attribute and AI-disclosure text required by current Etsy guidance, consistent with the asset record.

Disclose:

- Digital delivery.
- Sizes.
- Software.
- Inclusions.
- Limits.

Ground price in evidence and costs.

Avoid:

- Fake scarcity.
- Fake discounts.
- Invented reviews.
- Unsupported certification.

Create truthful demonstrations when useful.

Verify current title/tag guidance before preparing the listing.

Prefer concise, clear titles that identify the product and important traits.

Front-load information for buyer comprehension, not a promised ranking boost.

Use the available relevant tag slots with varied buyer phrases and verify character limits.

Do not ban a relevant phrase merely because it also appears in the title.

Avoid redundant tags and unnecessary exact category/attribute duplication.

Consult Etsy's official Keywords 101 and listing guidance when resolving rules.

Show:

- The actual product.
- Useful filled-in examples.
- Readable detail views.

Use contextual mockups only when they truthfully represent what the buyer receives.

Clearly distinguish digital files from physical goods.

Keep illustrative data visibly labelled.

Match functional and compatibility claims to the verification log.

Distinguish illustrative calculations, tested behaviour and untested intended benefits.

Do not turn a proposed benefit into a demonstrated result.

Do not publish without authorisation.

Verify applicable rules before stating refund/legal obligations.

Do not describe decorative marriage stationery as a valid legal contract or finance tools as tax-compliant without verification.

### Validate and iterate when requested

Define:

- Hypothesis.
- Offer.
- Traffic source.
- Budget.
- Observation period.
- Continue/revise/stop criteria.

Do not invent universal conversion thresholds.

Interpret sales with relevant visits and uncertainty.

Date major changes.

Do not call small observational tests controlled causal proof.

Expand related products after evidence supports the core offer.

## PART 5 — HALAL SUITABILITY AND ORIGINALITY

Screen intended use, content and marketing, not Islamic branding alone.

Include suitable general-purpose tools.

Exclude products explicitly designed to promote:

- Gambling.
- Pornography.
- Intoxicants.
- Interest-based lending.
- Other clearly prohibited uses.

Assess neutral recordkeeping by function. Do not equate ordinary income/expense tools with prohibited financial products.

Avoid:

- Deception.
- Fake reviews.
- Copied content.
- Misleading discounts.

Record user guidance on:

- Imagery.
- Audio/music.
- Celebrations.
- Sacred text.
- Disputed matters.

Do not:

- Silently choose a school of thought.
- Issue a fatwa.
- Prohibit all illustrations.
- Certify a business halal.

Use suitable neutral alternatives for reversible drafts.

Seek qualified guidance for material unresolved questions before inclusion.

Do not block unrelated suitable research for a disputed optional feature.

Verify Islamic quotations, Arabic, attribution and context with authoritative sources and qualified review as appropriate.

Do not invent:

- Hadith authenticity.
- Religious benefits.
- Rulings.

Verify assumptions and obtain appropriate review for religious calculations such as zakat.

Check respectful handling of sacred text.

Record:

- Asset creator/source.
- Licence.
- Relevant acquisition date.
- Permissions for the exact distribution format.

Commercial use does not automatically permit redistributing fonts, stock assets or editable templates.

Check tool-specific licences.

Do not claim exclusivity for nonexclusive or AI-generated assets.

Do not assume customisation, flattening or delivery through a template link cures licence restrictions.

Verify the specific asset and platform terms for the actual end product.

Assess educational and artistic content individually rather than declaring an entire category permissible without reviewing it.

Record each product's intended production method (original, licensed, AI-assisted, AI-generated) at shortlisting. Verify current Etsy creativity, intellectual-property and AI-disclosure rules for that method and product type at shortlisting, and re-check before finalisation. Carry any required AI disclosure into the listing package.

Resale/PLR permission alone does not establish Etsy eligibility or seller originality.

Do not reproduce competitors under an "improvement" label.

Flag material unresolved legal questions for appropriate advice.

This screen is not legal clearance.

Use statuses:

- Suitable under recorded requirements.
- Suitable subject to conditions.
- Requires qualified review.
- Exclude.

Give reasons and sources as appropriate.

Do not let demand outweigh a suitability failure.

Apply each status to the reviewed product, feature and version under recorded requirements.

Do not imply certification of the shop or business.

Keep the screen proportional to actual content and intended use.
